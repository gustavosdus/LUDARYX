ENGLISH

LUDARYX is a free, open-source unified game launcher for Windows.

Bring installed games from Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Xbox / Microsoft Store, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net and Riot Client into one library. LUDARYX includes desktop and TV modes, keyboard and controller navigation, artwork, metadata, local play statistics and a SHA-256 validated updater.
